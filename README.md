Poem: To the Bugs That Would Not Die

I coded a Streamlit app, everything just right,

Hit “deploy,” and prepared for delight.

But the page loaded with a sneer on its face,

Bright red errors all over the place.

No worries, I thought, it’s just a glitch.

But then it threw a traceback snitch.

“KeyError: ‘df’ not found.”

I swear I just... it was... around?

Edited the text, tried another trick,

Reasoned with myself like a lunatic. 

Refreshed the app, it crashed once more,

Then rebooted with new bugs I’d never seen before.

Loops inside loops, like Matryoshka dolls, 

They made sense last Tuesday, now not at all. 

I screamed into the void, “what’s wrong with me?”

The void responded, “ask ChatGPT.” 

Believe me, I did, but it doesn’t have a clue.

“Check your directory” — as if that’s something new.

“Perhaps it’s you who’s bugged, not the coding you do.”

I closed the tab and murmured, “yeah… that’s true.”

Somewhere inside, there’s a glimmer, a spark,

A hope that madness is part of the arc.

For one glorious moment, the app loaded just right…

Then crashed again. Welp. Good night.
